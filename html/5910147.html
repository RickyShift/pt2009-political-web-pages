Magellan Maestro 4700 Navigator Specs and Prices Announced [Maestro 4700 PND With Voice Command and Bluetooth]

Posted July 6th 2009 at 10:02 pm by Alex Ion | No Comments

To make sure you don’t miss any turns or highway exits this summer, Magellan have just taken the wraps off of their new Maestro 4700 Navigator. According to an announcement today, the new Magellan Maestro 4700 will boast voice command, hands-free Bluetooth calling (with a noise-canceling microphone) and a predictive traffic mode to analyze your past traffic patterns that will help suggest alternate routes (keeps you away from slow points).

Among the features that will probably make it a hot seller, the new Magellan Maestro 4700 is going to pack a a 4.7-inch touchscreen, will feature a double-capacity OneTouch favorites menu for more frequently-visited destination, both standard POI and AAA TourBook databases. It also comes complete with the new “Find Your Car” feature with pedestrian mode, 3D Landmarks and a QuickSpell with Smart City Search.

The new Magellan Maestro 4700 is expected to hit stores starting July 15 with a windshield mount and cradle, an USB cable, a pouch and on-dash adhesive mounting disc. It should retail for a MSRP of $299.
