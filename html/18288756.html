Contest - Largehearted Housecleaning x2 - Win 50 CDs + 50 Books

With the first Largehearted Boy housecleaning contest prize pack in the mail this morning, my book and CD shelves are a bit lighter, but not enough.

So this week's contest will double the previous prize and offer 50 books and 50 CDs to one lucky Largehearted Boy reader.

To enter the contest, leave a comment in this post and tell me the best book or CD gift you have ever received.

The winner will receive an assortment of 50 CDs and 50 books chosen randomly from my collection. Some may be great, some not-so-great, but you should find something worthwhile in the bunch (and hopefully others to pass on to your friends and family).

The winner will be chosen randomly at midnight CT Friday evening (June 26th).
