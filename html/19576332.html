Space Sound Records releases two new cd's

2009-01-26 written by Lauri

Space Sound Records releases two new albums. Synthesizer Tribute to Depeche Mode has 14 Depeche Mode tracks remixed in synthesizer and spacesynth style. It features many classic DM tracks such as Black Celebration, Stripped and Enjoy the Silence. Some of the tracks are purely instrumental, some feature vocoders and some have clean vocals to preserve the real DM feeling.

Tracklist:

1. Galaxy Hunter - Black Celebration
2. Marco Rochowski - Just Can't Get Enough
3. Vanello - New Life
4. Dreamtime - Never Let Me Down Again
5. Cyber Space - Strangelove
6. Space Project - Photographic
7. Galaxy Hunter - Behind the Wheel
8. Krzystof Radomski - Rush
9. Staffan Öhman - No Disco
10. Marco Rochowski - Enjoy the Silence
11. Vocoderion - Stripped
12. Vanello - Puppets
13. Galaxy Hunter - Everything Counts
14. A.M Samurai - The Things You Said



The other brand new cd The Best of Spacesound Records features 14 tracks from well known spacesynth artists.

Tracklist:

1. A.M Samurai - Secret Entrance
2. Vanello - Fly, Rocket!
3. Marco Rochowski - Orbit of Fire (Remix)
4. Space Project - Abandoned Places
5. Starlight - Moon Express
6. Galaxy Hunter - Silver Moon
7. Dreamtime - Escape to the Past
8. Vocoderion - Intergalactic Race
9. Vanello - Planet Synth
10. Marco Rochowski - Cosmic Renaissance (Remix)
11. Anders Lundqvist - The Search For Otherland
12. Staffan Ohman - Mission To Accomplish
13. Prodomo - Black Hole
14. Prometheus - Eudaimonia

More info at www.spacesoundrecords.com
