Enchufada

Enchufada Record label is the jamboree from a bunch of creative guys, who love to eat, sleep, have fun and on the way to the Milky Way, create new tunes to the world.

These guys go by the names of João Barbosa A.k.a. João Buraka (beats IQ), Kalaf Angelo (words IQ) and Zumbi Ya A.k.a Tupac (have it all IQ).

Our guts is to wegue wegue and venture tunes that design a sound axis across the urban centres of Africa, Europe and U.S. by upholding fair trade deals with artists.

Behind Enchufada is a cluster of talented people, who whispered the foundations of our philosophy and still muse with us:

Inês Valdez A.K.a Maivaldez (Agent I.Q.) Vasco Chaparro (designer) Ana Borges (office manager) Carlos Monteiro (Promotion Manager) Chris Dressler (EaE CEO) and his team of programmers

Among many others.
